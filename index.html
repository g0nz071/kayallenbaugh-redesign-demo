About Kay
"While I paint, I focus on my mission to bring beauty into the world that did not exist before."

Kay lives in Lake Oswego, Oregon, with her husband Eric and their two dogs, Buddy and Lucy. Inspiration feeds her soul, whether from art or books — the same joy that drove her best-selling Chocolate series carries into every watercolor she paints today.
